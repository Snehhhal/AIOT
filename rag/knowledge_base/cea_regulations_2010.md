# Central Electricity Authority (Measures Relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2010

## Regulation 19: Handling of Electric Supply Lines and Apparatus
1. Before any conductor or apparatus is handled, it shall be isolated from any live conductor by an approved disconnecting device.
2. The isolated conductor or apparatus shall be tested with an approved voltage testing device to prove that it is de-energized.
3. The isolated conductor or apparatus shall be efficiently connected to earth before commencing work.
4. If a leakage current is detected exceeding statutory thresholds, the structure shall be treated as energised until isolation and earthing are confirmed by authorized personnel.

## Regulation 35: Danger Notices and Public Warnings
1. The owner of every medium, high, and extra-high voltage installation shall affix permanently in a conspicuous position a danger notice in Hindi, English, and the local language.
2. In the case of distribution poles where continuous leakage monitoring indicates insulation breakdown, automated audible and visual warning systems shall activate immediately to alert pedestrians.

## Regulation 43: Isolation, Grounding and Earth Fault Interruption
1. Every overhead utility pole supporting low or medium voltage distribution conductors shall have its metallic chassis bonded to earth with an earth resistance not exceeding 5.0 Ohms.
2. Where touch voltage or earth potential rise exceeds 50 Volts during wet or waterlogged ground conditions, automatic disconnection of supply must occur within statutory time limits (not exceeding 200 milliseconds).
3. If automatic disconnection fails or is bypassed, an immediate minimum safety perimeter of 5.0 meters shall be enforced by barricading or perimeter notification.

## Regulation 46: Protection Against Leakage Current and Human Life Safety
1. All low and medium voltage installations shall be protected by an earth leakage protective device capable of operating when the leakage current exceeds 30 milliamperes.
2. The 30 milliamperes limit represents the international physiological threshold for ventricular fibrillation and muscular tetanization (let-go threshold) under IEC 60479-1.
3. No worker shall enter standing water within 5 meters of an energized utility structure without certified dielectric protection.
