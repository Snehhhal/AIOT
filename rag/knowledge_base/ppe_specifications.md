# Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for Electrical Utility Linemen

## 1. Dielectric Insulating Gloves (IS 4770 / ASTM D120)
* Mandatory Specification: Class 0 Dielectric Insulating Rubber Gloves rated for working voltages up to 1000 Volts AC (proof test voltage: 5000 Volts AC).
* Inspection: Must be air-tested for punctures, pinholes, and chemical degradation immediately prior to each field shift.
* Wear over-gloves made of premium leather to provide mechanical tear and puncture protection.

## 2. Dielectric Safety Boots and Overshoes (EN 50321 / IS 15298)
* Mandatory Specification: Non-metallic, non-sparking electrical hazard composite-toe safety boots with dielectric vulcanized rubber outsole rated to 18 kV.
* In waterlogged monsoon conditions: Wear waterproof dielectric overshoes covering up to calf height to resist electrical leakage conductivity in standing puddles.

## 3. Arc Flash & Head Protection (IS 2925 / NFPA 70E)
* Mandatory Specification: Type 1, Class E non-conductive electrical safety helmet with chin strap.
* Face Shield: 8 cal/cm² arc-rated polycarbonate anti-fog face shield attached to the safety helmet.
* Safety Eyewear: UV-filtering safety glasses complying with ANSI Z87.1.

## 4. Voltage Testing & Grounding Gear
* Certified capacitive non-contact AC voltage testing stick (operating range: 100V to 33kV).
* Portable 3-phase short-circuiting and earthing set with heavy-duty serrated copper clamps.
